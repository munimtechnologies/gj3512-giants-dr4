# Scoop check, 2026-11-22

Searches (web, arXiv listings): "GJ 3512" with Gaia / astrometric orbit / true mass / 2026.

- No published astrometric orbit or directly measured true mass for GJ 3512 b or c.
- Latest constraint: Morales et al. 2025 (arXiv:2507.15516, Section 6): masses about 0.78
  (+0.04/-0.27) and 0.77 (+0.04/-0.26) M_Jup from the Gaia DR3 excess noise (0.264 mas) under a
  coplanar assumption; i about 36 (+27/-1.2) deg, Omega about 142.5 deg. They state DR4 epoch
  astrometry will overcome the caveats.
- Piccinini et al. 2026 (arXiv:2601.09401, RV + Hipparcos-Gaia true masses) do not include GJ 3512.
- Gaia DR3: non_single_star = 0 (no DR3 orbital solution), RUWE 1.25.
- Gaia DR4 release confirmed for 2026-12-02 12:00 CET (ESA Gaia news).

Authors worth telling once graded: Juan Carlos Morales, Ignasi Ribas, Alexander J. Mustill,
Melvyn B. Davies, Ansgar Reiners (CARMENES), Javier Lopez-Santiago; for Gaia exoplanet
astrometry context: Alessandro Sozzetti, Berry Holl, Johannes Sahlmann.
